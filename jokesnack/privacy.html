Privacy Policy

Joke Snack. Last updated: 8 June 2026.

This policy describes how Joke Snack (“the app”) handles information when you use it. If anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

The app is designed to be offline-first. Your preferences and how you use the app are stored on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the app.

We do not sell your personal information. Any analytics or diagnostics we use are not used to identify you personally and are handled in line with platform rules (for example, Apple’s App Store and privacy guidelines).

The app may open links or share content through other apps (for example, when you share a joke to social media). Those services have their own privacy practices; we are not responsible for their content or policies.

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
